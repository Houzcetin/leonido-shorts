Leonido Shorts

A small, self-hosted publishing tool used by a single creator to produce and upload vertical short videos to their own YouTube channel, GTA6 Leonido.

Uses YouTube API Services

What it does

  1. Collects gameplay clips (Grand Theft Auto VI) from Kick streamers who have given the channel owner written permission to repost their clips.
  2. Edits each clip into a 9:16 vertical video: the streamer's camera on top, gameplay below, a credit badge with the streamer's Kick channel, Turkish subtitles and the channel watermark.
  3. Shows every finished video in a local review panel. The channel owner manually approves or rejects each one; nothing is published without this approval.
  4. Uploads approved videos to the owner's own YouTube channel with the YouTube Data API (videos.insert), crediting the original streamer and linking to the source in the description.
Who can use it?
Leonido Shorts is not a public service. It runs only on the channel owner's computer and is used only to manage the owner's own YouTube channel. There are no other users, accounts or sign-ups.

YouTube data

The tool signs in with the channel owner's Google account via OAuth 2.0 and only requests the scopes needed to upload videos and read the owner's own channel name. See the Privacy Policy for details and the Terms of Service for the rules of use.
