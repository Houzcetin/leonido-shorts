Terms of Service

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Scope

Leonido Shorts is a private, self-hosted tool. It is operated and used only by the owner of the GTA6 Leonido YouTube channel to publish videos to that channel. It is not offered to the public.

2. YouTube Terms

The tool uses YouTube API Services. Use of the tool is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Content rules

  • Clips are only taken from Kick streamers who have given the channel owner written permission to repost them. Proof of permission is kept by the owner.
  • Every video credits the original streamer on screen and links to their channel and the original clip in the description.
  • Every video is reviewed and approved manually by the channel owner before it is uploaded. Clips with story spoilers or content that violates YouTube's Community Guidelines are rejected.
  • Uploads are rate-limited (a few videos per day) to stay within YouTube API quota and to avoid repetitive, mass-produced content.

4. Removal requests

Any streamer or rights holder who wants a video removed can request it via the project's GitHub issues page; the video will be taken down promptly.
